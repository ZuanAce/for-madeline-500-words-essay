Hi Madeline,

I want to be honest with you: I like you. Not only because you're beautiful, but because there's something about the way you speak, carry yourself, and move through the world that stays with me.

Being around you has made me want to grow—not to become someone you might approve of, but to become someone I'm proud to be. I find myself remembering little things you've shared: your love for music and dancing, discovering different styles, green tea lattes, board games, and those garden-style bouquets you like.

I admire your independence and the way you make your own choices. I don't want to change that or rush you. I'd simply like to get to know you more, share some laughs, go for a drive, try somewhere lovely to eat, and make good memories at a pace that feels right for both of us.

I know a feeling can't be requested or promised. You don't owe me the same answer, and I don't want this note to make things uncomfortable. Whatever you feel, I'll respect it.

If there's a part of you that might like to explore this too, I'd be happy to show up with patience, kindness, honesty, and consistency. Not just say pretty words, but let my actions speak over time.

For now, I just wanted you to know: I like you, genuinely. Thank you for being yourself.

— With warmth, and no pressure. ♡
